# Scene asset licences

Every third-party model, sky and texture under `public/scenes/` comes from a CC0 (public domain) source; the one exception is the Basis Universal texture decoder in `basis/`, under Apache-2.0. Models were resized, deduplicated, pruned and meshopt-compressed with gltf-transform; geometry and animation are otherwise unchanged. Textures were resized and baked to KTX2 (Basis Universal); the Courtyard, turf, mulch and insulation sets are generated by Tri Pros.
