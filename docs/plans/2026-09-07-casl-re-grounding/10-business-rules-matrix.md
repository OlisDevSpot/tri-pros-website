## 1. Principals and their business meaning

**super-admin** — The company's system administrators (Sean, and the `info@` system account). "System admin. Can delete, manage all" (UL:139). One rule, `can('manage','all')` (AB:96), compiles to no `WHERE` — omni is *emergent* from a conditionless rule, never a hand-coded `null` (CAT:87, SPEC:57-59). Super-admins gate global/admin operations (`superAdminProcedure`: lead-sources, voip-campaign resync/binding, `manage CustomerPipeline` moves, `assign Meeting` participant management — TRPC:64-79, CAT:91-93, AB:163). They see ungated phone (customers/DOCS.md:28). Two caveats the docs attach: pure-portfolio projects are filtered out of operational lists *even for omni*, but that is a business filter, not authorization (CAT:123, projects/DOCS.md:117-125); and the `info@` login is "a godmode super-admin [that] is NOT a person — it cannot be dispatched to a meeting, cannot be a sales agent" (meetings/DOCS.md:33-41).

**agent** — A Tri Pros sales rep. "Full sales + dashboard. Auto-assigned for `@triprosremodeling.com` signups" (UL:138; FC P0-20). Runs the in-home meeting, authors proposals, mints projects, works the `fresh` + `projects` pipelines (AB:162-164). The agent's reach over the whole entity graph derives from ONE fact — **meeting participation**: "the meeting is where the customer relationship is owned" (customers/DOCS.md:20-22; meetings/DOCS.md:3, 74-82; proposals/DOCS.md:74-78; #210). Agents never create customers ("office/super-admin responsibility", AB:104; CAT:104) — leads arrive from telemarketing/social via intake (`docs/company/overview.md:45-63`) and are booked by intake/dispatcher/super-admin. Agents `own` the meetings they create and thereby implicitly fill the sales-agent role (meetings/DOCS.md:24, 37; AB:134). Internal financials (cost lines, margin, multiplier) are agent-only and never visible to homeowners (UL:92-95; proposals/DOCS.md:297-299).

**dispatcher** — An internal lead-qualifier, "a person, e.g. an overseas VA, who converts leads → booked appointments; NOT a sales agent" (M-DISP:10; AB:220-224; DISP:5). Works the whole *operational* pipeline — `leads`, `rehash`, `dead` AND `fresh` (so a lead they just booked stays visible) but never `projects` ("converted = the agent's book", DISP:176; AB:229-233; user ruling 2026-08-20). Dials leads → ungated phone via the `LeadsPool` capability (M-DISP:13-15; CAT:159-162). Books meetings that land **system-owned / unassigned** because the role deliberately lacks `own Meeting` (M-DISP:17; AB:264; `meetings/lib/resolve-owner.ts:13-18`). Sees + edits the sales-discovery profile and works notes "exactly like an agent" (DISP:229-238; EPIC:298; M-SPLIT "User ruling: dispatchers see+edit the profile; only financials walled"). **Financial wall:** zero `Proposal` and zero `Project` rows — enforced by the *absence* of a grant, not by masking (EPIC:184; DISP:19, 25).

**homeowner — the share-token bearer (no session)** — The customer opening `?token=tpr-…` on a proposal. "The token IS the authorization for that read … Tokens are permanent — never rotated, never expired" (proposals/DOCS.md:42-48, 58-64). Reach is *exactly the shared row*: `{ kind:'token', scope: eq(proposals.token, t), subject:'Proposal' }`, "reach = one shared row, never null" (SPEC:56, 62; EPIC:116). The bearer may read AND make a small set of non-destructive updates (finance option, cash-in-deal, age via envelope context, "request agreement" signal) but "NEVER touches the contract lifecycle" (proposals/DOCS.md:219-222; proposal-flow/DOCS.md:83-90; TRPC:289). "Don't append authority beyond proposal read/update" (proposals/DOCS.md:404). Two smaller bearer kinds exist: the funnel `leadId` (a `customers.id` UUID used as a capability for funnel-lead address/enrichment writes — EPIC:116; FC P0-4/5) and the lead-source intake `{slug, token}` pair (lead-sources/DOCS.md:20-32; FC P1-13). A `voip-link-tokens` bearer is designed (voip-link-tokens/DOCS.md:34-44) but has no live route (FC P1-15).

**homeowner / user accounts (better-auth sessions)** — Real flows: **none today.** Google sign-in with a non-corporate address yields role `user`; `homeowner` is only assignable by DB edit or the Playwright dev route (FC P5 §1.6; `auth/server.ts:38-48,74`). The only procedures that serve them are push `subscribe`/`unsubscribe` (FC P5-1); every dashboard route rejects them (EPIC:124 — "the gate's real load-bearing job is excluding `homeowner`/`user` accounts"). AB:194-206 calls the homeowner grants "future-proofing for customer portal". The docs record a *load-bearing invariant* about them: **homeowner is ALWAYS `token`, never `user`** — if a homeowner session ever compiled its conditionless `read Proposal` as a user actor, "every proposal leaks" (SPEC:64; M-P5 "documented future-guard"). Today a signed-in homeowner-role account cannot even open its own share link (FC P5-3). UL:133-139 lists `user | homeowner | agent | super-admin` — the `dispatcher` row is missing (enum `src/shared/constants/enums/user.ts:1`; §6 X13).

**system** — Jobs, webhooks, cron, scripts, and public intake orchestrators: "the ONLY unrestricted actor, and it must say why" (SPEC:56-60). `systemActor(reason)` takes a closed, namespaced `SystemReason` union — `webhook:* | sync:* | intake:* | admin:*` — plus the `derived:*` transitive-write class; "the union IS the auditable catalog of every unrestricted write; adding a bypass is a deliberate reviewable act" (EPIC:117, 115). Today the union has two members, `derived:contract-age-from-token-proposal` and the legacy `legacy:system-context` (`scope/system-reasons.ts:8-15`); 30 bare `SYSTEM_CONTEXT` sites remain (RG §A.2, §F3). Sub-kinds as the docs use them: **intake** — funnels/landing/intake/Bina create customers + attribution + notes (+ a meeting owned by `info@`) as SYSTEM, "never through any authenticated agent mutation" (customers/DOCS.md:181-189; FC P0-3/7/11, P6-2); **webhook** — Zoho Sign contract events (viewed/completed→auto-approve/declined), QuickBooks, CloudTalk/JustCall dispositions, Google Calendar (proposals/DOCS.md:247-277; FC P6-2..6); **sync** — QStash jobs (GCal push, QB records/invoices, Zoho status, Meta CAPI, AI summary) (FC P6-1); **admin** — super-admin handlers that escalate to system (voip-campaign enrol/unenrol, `moveCustomerToPipeline`) and CLI scripts (FC §1.5, P6-7); **derived** — "a server-derived id from an already scope-authorized read … sound only because the preceding read is scope-enforced; target must never be client input" (EPIC:115; `system-reasons.ts:9-10`).

**Two future principals the docs reserve:** **manager** — "should see *all* meetings (not omni, not a participant)"; "the requirement that motivated this restructure"; becomes "4 conditionless lines in the Policy" (CAT:88, 230; SPEC:20, 66, 166-169; EPIC:344 E2E roster). **customer portal** (authenticated homeowner) — "we'll either add a condition here or use a join-based check" (AB:199-202).

## 3. The matrix

Columns: **Row condition** (prose) · **CASL shape** (what the rule would look like; `me` = `user.id`, `SYS` = system-account user id) · **Fields** · **Status** · **Source** · **Today** (grant in `AB`, or how the code gates it) · **Δ** (what changes when this is authored). "Bridge" = the structural `parent` child-bridge (`fk IN (SELECT parent.pk WHERE <parent scope>)`) — the child rule stays a conditionless verb and inherits the parent's *read* scope (EPIC:190; RG §C3 shape (a) for own-column conditions).

## 4. Cross-cutting invariants (each cited)

1. **Financial wall = absent `Proposal`/`Project` grants ⇒ zero rows (row-level deny, no field masking).** "Dispatcher sees NO proposal rows at all (not 'sees the row, money blanked')" (EPIC:184); "Financial subjects unchanged: dispatcher gets NO `read Proposal` / `read Project` grant. That absence is the wall" (DISP:19, 25). The `hasSentProposal` existence badge is masked for non-Proposal actors (DISP:56-70). The 6 Ledger-1 cross-entity financial reads must go through the owning DAL (EPIC:198-208; RG §E1-5, §E2). For the **token bearer** the wall is *field-level*: cost lines / margin / multiplier are "internal/agent-only … Never visible to homeowner" (UL:92-95; proposals/DOCS.md:297-299, 405) — §5 G3.
2. **Existence-non-leak.** "Writes + single-row-by-id fetches with a client-supplied id: point-probe `canAccess(spec, actor, id, action)` → throw `NOT_FOUND` on miss (not FORBIDDEN — don't leak existence), scoped write retained underneath as defense-in-depth. Lists/filters stay pure scope-threaded" (EPIC:112-114). Activities' `FORBIDDEN` responses are a ledgered residual to flip (EPIC:340). Row-miss inside a scoped read IS the point-probe (EPIC:283).
3. **Homeowner-always-token; bearer reach = its own row(s) only.** `token` actor = `{ scope: eq(tokenColumn, t), subject }`, "reach = one shared row, never null" (SPEC:56, 62); "homeowner is ALWAYS `token`, never `user` … If a homeowner ever resolved as `kind:'user'`, `read Proposal` would compile to allow-all → every proposal leaks" (SPEC:64). "`tokenActor` = scope + subject, NO ability. Verb-boundary is the *endpoint*; row-boundary is the scope … id-as-capability only on random-UUID pks, never `unsafeId` serial" (EPIC:116). "Token IS authorization; CASL is `null`" (proposals/DOCS.md:60, 400; TRPC:172, 366) — the RG §J2 decision (abilityless helper vs a purpose-built homeowner ability) reopens *how* this is expressed, not the reach.
4. **Agent-first on shareable routes.** "Session is checked BEFORE token: authenticated ⇒ `userActor` (token in URL ignored); else token ⇒ `tokenActor`; else reject … a token no longer sideways-grants an authenticated agent a row outside their CASL scope" (EPIC:300; TRPC:171-175). Consequence today: a signed-in `homeowner`-role account cannot open its own link (FC P5-3) — acceptable while no portal exists.
5. **Phone visibility threshold.** "Agents see a customer's phone number only when the customer has a proposal at status `sent` or `approved`. Omni + leads-pool (dispatcher) + trusted server/token callers see it ungated. Phone is null-gated at the DAL layer (`gatedPhoneSql`), not by hiding in the UI" (customers/DOCS.md:26-35; M-PHONE:12 — threshold, not equality). Per-row threshold stays a SQL `CASE` (depends on sibling-table state); the **bypass** is CASL (`can('read','Customer','phone')` for omni / `LeadsPool` / token) — `mask = caslUngated ? raw : thresholdCase` (CAT:103; SPEC:281-285). `LeadsPool` stays ONE named capability (EPIC:239 B — supersedes CAT Fork B).
6. **Meeting ownership ≠ participation.** "`meetings.ownerId` is a permission level, not a meeting role. It answers 'who can delete/fully-edit this meeting?' The owner is the user who created the meeting record … Only the owner OR a super-admin can delete" (meetings/DOCS.md:19-29, 178-183). Participant roles are meeting-contextual functions; the `owner` participant role is being removed (#217, PLANNED — meetings/DOCS.md:43-57); the per-role permission matrix is #220 (open design); owner-delete is #218 → sequence **#217 → #220 → Meeting update/delete rows** (RG §D2). Participant add/remove/promote cardinality is a state machine gated by the `assign` verb, "do not fold into the row engine" (CAT:91).
7. **Dispatcher-booked meetings are system-owned (unassigned).** "If info@ (system account) creates → info@ is owner. The meeting has no implicit sales agent" (meetings/DOCS.md:23, 35); the dispatcher role "deliberately WITHOUT `can('own','Meeting')`, so meetings they create are owned by the system account" (AB:222-224; M-DISP:17; `resolve-owner.ts:13-18`); "input ownerId never trusted for authed callers" (M-DISP:17; DISP 2026-07-09:20); info@ is never seated as a participant (M-DISP:19; meetings crud `create.after`). This is exactly why the dispatcher `read Meeting` had to become unconditional (DISP:189-192).
8. **Project row-security = `participation OR ownerId=me`; `isPublic` is NOT authz.** "RULING (user 2026-08-11): Project row-security = participation OR ownerId=me. `isPublic=true` is dropped from the authz predicate — it stays a display/public-query filter only" (EPIC:248; AB:145-150); pure-portfolio rows are a business filter beside the scope (CAT:123). ⚠️ projects/DOCS.md:107-115 states a *different* canonical rule (participation with non-negative meeting outcome, no `ownerId`) — §6 X2, §5 G8.
9. **Create-side rules = point-probe of the parent's READ scope (never a `create` probe on the parent).** "'Can this actor create a child under parent X?' is a probe against the parent's READ scope … Probing with `action:'create'` would be wrong — an agent has no `create Customer` rule … it would deny every legitimate profile upsert" (SPEC:242-249). The create slot must gain this probe for every child (RG §C4; the open IDOR class RG §E1-4). Parent map: **Meeting ← Customer(read)**; **Proposal ← Meeting(read)**; **Application ← Meeting(read)**; **CustomerNote / CustomerProfile / (VoipCall, VoipMessage — implied) ← Customer(read)**; **ProposalMediaFile ← Proposal(read + `update Proposal` verb)**; **MediaFile ← Project(read + verb per §5 G12)**; **Project (operational) ← Meeting(read) + ≥1 proposal** (projects/DOCS.md:47); **ProposalView ← the token's Proposal**; **Activity ← none (owner-stamped)**; **Customer ← none (system/super-admin only)**.
10. **Transitive system writes.** "A server-derived id (from an already scope-authorized read …) → `systemActor(reason)` transitive write. Invariant: sound *only because* the preceding read is scope-enforced; target must never be client input" (EPIC:115; SPEC:268-274; `system-reasons.ts:9-10`). Every `SYSTEM_CONTEXT` site is classified A (mis-scoped user write — fix, don't wrap) / B (genuinely privileged) / C (bearer) *before* it is renamed (EPIC:111).
11. **Two future roles.** *manager* = conditionless `read` on the roots, "sees all, not omni" — the case the old `visibility(userId)` model could not express (CAT:88; SPEC:20, 166-169); *customer portal* = an authenticated homeowner whose `Proposal` reach must be a condition or a token, never conditionless (AB:199-202; SPEC:64).
12. **Migration invariant (extended to mutations).** "Conditions REPLACE the conditionless verb — never sit beside it. CASL OR-merges rules; a leftover conditionless `can('read', X)` dominates → allow-all → leak" (EPIC:74-77); "audit that no conditionless `can(update|delete, X)` survives beside a conditional one" (RG §D3). Child own-column conditions (`{authorId}`) compile via RG §J1 shape (a) — the current child branch would silently drop them (EPIC:50-52).
13. **State-machine transitions stay imperative (axis 5), never row conditions.** Meeting participant cardinality (CAT:91); proposal lock ladder incl. the share-token path (proposals/DOCS.md:194-232); application `draft`-only autosave and one-way submit/withdraw (applications/DOCS.md:16-24, 55-58); outcome derivation rules and the single outcome-change controller (meetings/DOCS.md:109-133); the conversion trigger (proposals/DOCS.md:90-104).
14. **Content flags and business filters are not authorization.** `proposal_media_files.visibility` (CAT:114); `projects.isPublic` (EPIC:248); pure-portfolio exclusion (CAT:123); `hasSentProposal` for phone-unlock UI (customers/DOCS.md:30). They live in read paths / view-models, not in the Policy — except that for the *bearer* the `homeowner` media flag is the row filter (proposals/DOCS.md:382).
