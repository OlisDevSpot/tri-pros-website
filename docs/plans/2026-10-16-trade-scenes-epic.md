### 2b. TS3b.6 findings: the reference render (2026-10-30)

The owner's target look is an AI render of a SoCal single-story flat-roof stucco home with an attached garage on a full suburban lot: `docs/plans/assets/trade-scene-realism/reference-render.jpg` (today's lab page beside it: `today-2026-10-30.jpg`). Three read-only research passes compared it with the scene; the full write-up (inventory of the render with sizes, the gap against the code with file:line, sourced lot dimensions, realism techniques and their iPad cost) is `docs/plans/2026-10-30-trade-scene-realism-research.md`. These are Claude's findings; the owner's rulings from them are R129–R132.

- **What makes the render read as real**, most first: a soft, low golden-hour sun with shade lit by the sky (never black); haze with distance; a dense, layered background (tile roofs, chimneys, palms, eucalyptus; no empty sky between them); darkening wherever things meet (wall bases, under each cornice step, the garage door recess); a lawn that varies; imperfect hard surfaces; trim with depth (stepped cornice, sills past the casings, recessed garage door, parapet cap); a believable lot (property walls, gate, path, driveway, car, patio); varied planting (agave, sago, pygmy date palms in mulch beds with steel edging); a gentle film-like grade.
- **The biggest gaps**, by visual impact: (1) no lot or surroundings: the house sits on a 60 m green disc with one back wall, no side walls, gate, street or neighbours; (2) midday front light: sun about 50° up, a two-panel 64 px environment, no ambient occlusion, no haze; (3) no textures: every house and yard surface is a flat matte colour; (4) no facade trim: no cornice, window surrounds or stoop on a slab home, white frames; (5) no garage: no input and no geometry, so the driveway ends at nothing (R80); (6) landscaping is two lollipop trees and 11 shrubs; (7) the camera is low and telephoto (fov 30, about 24 m out) against the render's drone view.
- **Lot defaults** (sourced, metres; to confirm in the brainstorm): lot 18.3 × 33.5 for an older single-story tract, 15.2 × 30.5 for a 1990s+ two-story one; setbacks front 6.1, each side 1.52, rear 4.57; property walls 1.83 and a front fence at most 1.07 (R130); two-car garage door 4.88 × 2.13, one-car 2.74 × 2.13; two-car garage 6.1 × 6.1; driveway 5.49 wide; sidewalk 1.52, parkway 2.13, curb 0.15, street 11.0 curb to curb. The render itself reads as a house about 12 × 7.5 m, set about 7–8 m back, with a 5.5 m driveway.
- **The cheap path to the look on an iPad** (research; no full-screen effects): a self-hosted 1k golden-hour sky image (Poly Haven; drei's `preset` skies download at runtime, which an in-home connection can break); a warm low sun with AgX or Neutral tone mapping; shadows drawn once and redrawn only when the house changes (`BakeShadows`, since the canvas renders on demand); haze matched to the horizon; neighbours merged into one or two draw calls; the lot at real scale; world-scaled UVs on the code-built boxes with 1k compressed (KTX2) stucco, block, concrete and grass textures (about 25–35 MB of GPU memory, decoder self-hosted); palms, agaves and sagos built in code and instanced (no light free models of them were found). Estimate: about 70–80 draw calls. Ambient occlusion (N8AO) only as an optional last step: at rest, half resolution, off in the ghost and Thermal. Trap if a post-processing composer is ever added: it turns the renderer's tone mapping off, and its default 4× multisampling on half-float buffers adds about 180 MB on a 12.9" iPad at dpr 2, enough for Safari to kill the tab.
- **Texture traps in our code**: maps simply drop out under the Thermal lens (fine), but the decal rule `transparent && map` would paint a ghosted textured wall as a decal (key decals off an explicit tag instead); alpha-cutout plants would paint as solid cards; skinned models bypass the thermal shader; `wornColor` keeps working only if colour maps stay neutral grey (the hue stays in `color`); `LoadedFurniturePiece` forces roughness and zero metalness, which flattens realistic models; merged boxes carry per-box UVs, so tiled textures would stretch.
- **Split by file**, to stay out of the TS3b.4a fixes, the Attic plan, paint wear (R121) and TS3b.4b: **6-A, can start now** in files nobody else holds: lighting, sky and haze; the lot, side yards, street and neighbours; landscaping; model loading; the thermal decal and alpha fix; world UVs. **6-B, after that work lands**: house textures and trim, the parapet cornice, window surrounds, the garage (R80, R129) and camera framing.
